# Clinical Reference: Emergency Triage Protocols and Red Flag Symptoms

**Source:** Emergency Medicine Clinical Practice Standards & National Triage Systems  
**Category:** Emergency Medicine / Clinical Triage  
**Target Audience:** Clinical Triage and Medical AI Assistant Response Protocols  

---

## 1. Critical Red Flag Emergencies Requiring Immediate 108 / 911 Dispatch

Whenever a patient presents with any of the following signs, the medical assistant must immediately issue an urgent directive to seek emergency medical attention or call emergency medical services (108 in India, 911 in North America):

1. **Suspected Acute Coronary Syndrome / Heart Attack:**
   - Sudden onset substernal chest heaviness, pressure, or tightness radiating to the jaw, neck, back, or left arm.
   - Associated with diaphoresis (cold sweats), shortness of breath, nausea, or dizziness.
2. **Acute Stroke (FAST Protocol):**
   - **F - Face:** Sudden facial droop or asymmetric smile.
   - **A - Arms:** Sudden unilateral arm or leg weakness or numbness.
   - **S - Speech:** Slurred speech, word-finding difficulty, or inability to comprehend speech.
   - **T - Time:** Immediate emergency dispatch; stroke therapies (thrombolysis/thrombectomy) are highly time-critical.
3. **Severe Respiratory Distress:**
   - Inability to speak in full phrases due to breathlessness, audible stridor, cyanosis (blue lips or nail beds), SpO2 < 90%.
4. **Anaphylaxis:**
   - Rapidly progressing acute allergic reaction involving two or more organ systems (e.g., hives + tongue/lip swelling + wheezing or hypotension). Immediate intramuscular epinephrine (0.3 mg IM in mid-outer thigh) required.
5. **Severe Hyperglycemic or Hypoglycemic Crisis:**
   - Unresponsiveness, generalized seizures, severe confusion, or deep rapid breathing in a person with known diabetes.

## 2. Adult Normal Vital Sign Reference Ranges

- **Heart Rate (Pulse):** 60 - 100 beats per minute (bpm) at rest.
  - Tachycardia: > 100 bpm.
  - Bradycardia: < 60 bpm (may be physiological in trained athletes).
- **Blood Pressure:** Systolic 90 - 120 mmHg, Diastolic 60 - 80 mmHg.
  - Hypotension: Systolic < 90 mmHg or Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) < 65 mmHg.
- **Respiratory Rate:** 12 - 20 breaths per minute.
  - Tachypnea: > 20 bpm (sensitive marker for respiratory or metabolic distress).
- **Body Temperature:** 36.5 - 37.5 deg C (97.7 - 99.5 deg F) orally.
  - Fever (Pyrexia): >= 38.0 deg C (100.4 deg F).
- **Oxygen Saturation (SpO2):** 95% - 100% on ambient room air at sea level.

## 3. Communication Guidelines for AI Medical Chatbots
1. **Safety First:** Never provide definitive diagnoses or alter prescribed medication dosages.
2. **Empathy with Clarity:** Provide clear, jargon-free explanations, organized with bullet points.
3. **Context Sensitivity:** When patient-specific allergy or medication profiles are available, factor them into risk considerations without violating medical privacy or confusing clinical guidelines.
